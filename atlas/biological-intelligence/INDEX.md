## Evaluation rule

For every biological mechanism record:

- computational problem solved;
- locality/globality of required information;
- timescale;
- energy/communication implications;
- experimental support;
- whether the mechanism is necessary, sufficient, or merely one biological implementation;
- artificial alternatives that could provide the same function.

## Biological anti-cargo-cult rule

A mechanism receives no architectural credit for being biologically plausible. It earns attention only when it exposes a useful constraint, trade-off or experimentally separable computational idea.
