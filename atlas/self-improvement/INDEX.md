# Self-Improvement — Coupled Map

**Status:** first evidence pass in progress.

Self-improvement is the problem of converting observed weaknesses/opportunities into validated changes to the correct layer of the system without allowing the improvement process to corrupt its own objective, evaluator or recovery path.

## Shared evaluation axes

Every candidate self-improvement mechanism should report:

- target substrate and mutation scope;
- diagnosis/credit evidence linking weakness to target layer;
- proposal diversity and search cost;
- improvement on target metrics;
- hidden/holdout and adversarial regressions;
- transfer beyond the triggering task;
- resource/latency changes;
- rollback/recovery ability;
- evaluator/control integrity after mutation;
- effect on future data/trace distribution;
- lifetime benefit after repeated reuse;
- diversity/open-endedness versus premature convergence.

## Anti-assumption

Do not equate self-improvement with weight training, code self-rewriting, recursive depth or autonomous removal of constraints. A system improves only when a proposed change produces independently validated net benefit over the relevant future distribution and does not invalidate the machinery used to judge or recover from that change.
