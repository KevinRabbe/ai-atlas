# Provisional selections

## PS-001 — Typed hybrid boundary state
Exact identity, control and provenance remain exact. Tolerant numerical/learned state may use compact approximate representations; version/integrity stay explicit.

## PS-002 — Staged adaptive persistence
New evidence normally enters reversible/tentative state before globally durable mutation. Consolidation timescale responds to stability/confidence and can buy independent corroboration when change-source uncertainty matters.

## PS-003 — Coupling-scoped coordination
Keep decisions/local organization local while dependencies/scarcity are local; expand coordination only to the scope where choices become coupled. Relation state itself is now subject to this rule: I26D shows a full evidence-dependence graph need not be materialized when assurance only touches sparse active pairs.

## PS-004 — Derived current belief with evidence linkage
Maintain cheap current belief while retaining source linkage whenever correction, contradiction, provenance, audit, rematerialization or dependence inference has meaningful expected value.

## PS-005 — Value-of-computation stopping
Continue optional inference only while estimated marginal downstream value exceeds compute, latency, risk and opportunity cost.

## PS-006 — Consequence-sensitive hypothesis plurality
Preserve multiple plausible states while ambiguity × consequence justifies their cost; prune as evidence resolves ambiguity or stakes fall. Unsupported execution/evidence states may remain explicitly unresolved.

## PS-007 — Value-driven active evidence acquisition
Acquire observations/interventions when expected downstream or epistemic value exceeds interaction cost/risk. This includes dependence/provenance/stress probes when uncertainty about source independence can change assurance value.

## PS-008 — Verified epistemic frontier expansion
Bootstrap knowledge is a starting prior/method library, not a permanent epistemic ceiling. Novel candidates remain tentative until sufficiently independent evidence supports promotion.

I26C sharpens the discovery implication: absence of observed dependence on familiar/resolved data does not prove independence on a new frontier.

## PS-009 — Conditional sharing with isolation fallback
Share learned computation while reusable structure creates transfer benefit; retain private/directional paths where interference or one-way dependence outweighs reciprocal sharing.

## PS-010 — Joint adaptive resource substitution under shared scarcity
When operations/resources materially substitute, complement or contend, allocation accounts jointly enough for their interaction to avoid locally rational but globally wasteful choices.

## PS-011 — Retrieval by expected applicability / downstream value
Persistent evidence is selected for expected usefulness to the current decision/belief/action. Similarity remains a cheap candidate signal when it predicts applicability.

## PS-012 — Adaptive predictive-state breadth / recoverable optionality
Keep information hot in proportion to expected future value versus carrying/rematerialization cost; preserve recoverability for distinctions with meaningful option value. I26D reproduces the same law for evidence-relation state.

## PS-013 — Failure-mode-independent assurance
Additional evaluators/audits/evidence matter only to the extent that they add sufficiently different relevant failure modes.

I05C/I17 show copied records do not multiply evidence. I24–I27 remove the assumption that failure-mode independence is always known exactly beforehand.

## PS-014 — Consequence/uncertainty/resource-sensitive assurance allocation
Verification/audit/reconciliation effort scales with expected harm reduction, uncertainty, optimizer pressure and assurance cost. Dependence/provenance evidence is itself an allocatable assurance resource.

## PS-015 — Causal/eligibility-scoped delayed credit
Delayed outcomes update transitions that remain plausible contributors. I22/I23 require exact historical causal identity/version or replayable history after structural recovery rather than positional attribution.

## PS-016 — Failure-layer-targeted verification
Verification targets the layer that can still invalidate the transition/result. Process, outcome, provenance, authority, topology, publication, external execution, evidence dependence and transient validity are not assumed substitutable.

## PS-017 — Independent current/revocable capability authority
Categorical authority is enforced independently from the behavior proposing an effect. Historical execution can be recorded after revocation, but every fresh/retry effect re-checks current permission.

## PS-018 — Rotating independent self-change regression evidence
Evidence authorizing durable self-change stays sufficiently independent from the proposal/optimization path and refreshes coverage across changing state-space, failure modes and timescales.

## PS-019 — Resource-priced lineage diversity / variant optionality
Retain multiple self-improvement lineages only while expected future reuse, uncertainty reduction or stepping-stone value exceeds storage/evaluation/coordination cost.

## PS-020 — Evidence-scaled repair scope / minimal sufficient blast radius
Prefer the smallest reversible change whose inferred causal scope covers the defect; escalate only when recurrence/coupling evidence makes wider scope worth regression, assurance and rollback cost.

## PS-021 — Regularity-scaled structural encoding / local override fallback
Represent/mutate structure indirectly when a compact rule captures reusable organization; preserve local override/isolation where exceptions make shared mutation harmful.

## PS-022 — Event-scoped execution with consistency-triggered synchronization
Execute on relevant state/dependency events while consistency scope remains local. Synchronize only across the dependency/version scope and when a common view is required.

## PS-023 — Value/sensitivity-scaled fidelity allocation
Treat numerical/computational fidelity as a resource dimension. Approximation is acceptable while plausible propagated error cannot change enough downstream value; escalate/rematerialize when it can.

## PS-024 — Failure-isolated consequential transition publication
Prepare multi-step consequential changes in non-authoritative/reversible state when partial visibility can violate invariants. Publish only across a coherence boundary after required validation/current-authority/version checks.

I14 adds crash recovery from semantic publication identity/base fencing rather than process phase. I18 shows target numeric version may be unknowable at preparation and state value alone may not identify the transition; authoritative publication provenance therefore crosses the same coherence boundary as state.

The selected object is publication/failure-isolation semantics, not a WAL, transaction database, lock, consensus protocol or deployment pattern.

## PS-025 — Externally grounded effect recovery / execution-authority separation
Local intent, phase state, proposal confidence, record count and past approval do not establish that an external effect occurred.

Recover execution from effect-specific external evidence or receiver-recognized identity where consequence justifies it. Every new/retry effect requires current authority. If exact execution cannot be identified, preserve unresolved state and price retry versus abstention explicitly.

I17/I20/I21/I27 progressively harden the evidence side from known lineage to learned effective dependence and raw-record aggregation inside the common recovery coordinator.

## PS-026 — Learned / causally qualified evidence dependence
Treat independence between evidence sources as uncertain, revisable **relational state**.

Do not infer independent failure modes from:

- record count;
- source names;
- agreement alone;
- raw co-failure alone;
- a below-threshold learned score with insufficient observation support;
- absence of failures outside the distribution where the consequential claim will be made.

Infer dependence from outcome history, provenance and controlled/interventional evidence while conditioning on plausible common causes. Acquire explicit dependency evidence when uncertainty about independence can materially change assurance value and its expected benefit exceeds cost.

### I24 — hidden/changing ancestry
Visible source identity stays fixed while hidden shared failure ancestry changes. Passive co-failure learning recovers the relation; value-priced probes accelerate recovery.

### I25 — common-cause confounding
Hard tasks make unrelated evaluators fail together. Raw covariance therefore overstates ancestry; context-conditioned residual dependence improves utility and audit efficiency.

### I26A — domain/scoped relation
The same pair can be dependent for one claim family and independent for another. One global graph reaches only ~0.80 pair accuracy in the matched family; domain-scoped relation reaches ~0.98 and reduces unnecessary audits.

### I26B — direction matters
Shared/common-mode dependence and derivation are not identical. A child may inherit parent errors yet sometimes independently repair them. Symmetric collapse loses this provenance direction; learned `A -> child` structure nearly matches known directional provenance in the synthetic family.

`EvidenceDerivationModel` therefore remains separate from symmetric `EvidenceDependenceModel` for now.

### I26C — selective frontier dependence
Sources can look independent on the resolved historical distribution yet synchronize on an unresolved high-consequence frontier. Passive co-failure then fails by construction; scoped provenance/stress diagnostics can earn their cost without destroying ordinary diversity.

### I26D — relation-state economics
At 128 sources, dense materialization of all 8,128 possible pairs is inferior to scoped active relation caching under nontrivial storage cost. When storage is free, permanent caching can win; no fixed cache policy is selected.

### I27 — common-path integration
Exact lineage is optional. Unknown names do not become independent. Learned independence requires observation-supported confidence. `aggregate_binary_evidence()` combines one contribution per effective failure group before confidence reaches assurance.

For 10%-error sources:

```text
4 copied agreeing records / one group -> ~10% error
2 independent agreeing groups        -> <2% error
2 unknown names / unsupported relation -> ~10% error
```

The common recovery path can now aggregate raw external evidence internally, preventing record-count false precision from entering through a privileged upstream caller.

### Selected object

The selected object is **the semantic requirement that evidence independence can itself be unknown, scoped, stale, directional where derivation matters, learned and actively tested**.

It is not a selection of covariance clustering, Bayesian aggregation, causal discovery, a provenance graph, pairwise dense storage or any particular diagnostic mechanism.

### Current falsifiers

- directional/multi-hop evidence can be handled as well by a simpler symmetric representation after equal information cost;
- adversarial sources can reliably defeat both behavioral and provenance/interventional dependence evidence without another useful discriminator;
- sparse/delayed truth makes relation learning systematically cost more than the assurance harm it prevents;
- a unified causal evidence representation materially outperforms the current separated symmetric-dependence + directional-derivation representation without losing typed semantics.

The next discriminator is not another PS number. It is whether I26B's directional derivation can be integrated into generic evidence aggregation without turning a derivation edge into a simplistic discard/collapse rule, followed by multi-hop/cyclic derivation stresses.

## Selection rule

A design decision moves to provisional selection only after matched resources, structurally different evidence, targeted falsification/ablation, explicit costs/regressions and an updated falsifier.

“Current systems do it this way” remains feasibility evidence, not optimality evidence.
