## Phase 10 — Experimental reconstruction

**Active. First provisional design-principle selections reached on 2026-08-14.**

All six Tier-1 discriminator shapes are implemented: E01, E02, E03, E04, E05 and E09. Promotion-quality follow-ups now add E01B, E02B, E04B and E09B.

Current evidence depth:

- control topology: two structurally different coupling families + resource-capacity shift;
- cognitive integration: linear and nonlinear/compositional families + training-data shifts + partial sharing;
- representation: flat state and search/backtracking state + bandwidth shift + score-distribution shift + corruption/version tests;
- persistence: fixed staging and hidden-volatility adaptive staging under noisy observations.

## First Phase-10 provisional selections

The selection rule has been satisfied narrowly for two **principles**, not whole architecture families:

### PS-001 / DL-004 — typed hybrid boundary state

Exact identity/control/provenance fields remain exact; tolerant numerical state may use compact approximate/learned channels; version and integrity semantics remain explicit at changing boundaries; human-readable audit is generated when needed rather than being the mandatory hot-path representation.

### PS-002 / DL-009 — staged adaptive persistence

New evidence normally enters reversible/tentative state before globally durable mutation. Persistence thresholds/timescales may adapt to estimated environmental stability rather than being one global constant.

Both remain falsifiable and compatible with multiple Phase-9 architecture families.

## Highest-value unresolved decisions

- **DL-001 control topology:** E01/E01B favor coordination scope that follows coupling scope; E22 cross-resource metacontrol remains decisive before promotion.
- **DL-002 cognitive integration:** E02/E02B favor adaptive degree of sharing, but partial sharing has unmatched realized compute; compute-matched conditional sharing is required.
- **DL-003 evidence vs compressed current state:** E03 has baseline evidence but needs a second family/resource shift.
- **DL-005 inference budget:** E05 has baseline adaptive-compute evidence but needs OOD cost shifts and promotion-quality replication.

## Near-term order

1. E22 — cross-resource substitution among compute, memory, observation and verification under changing prices;
