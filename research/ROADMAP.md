## Phase 8 — Forget implementations

First-pass clean-sheet functional reconstruction completed on 2026-08-14. **Exit gate: PASS.** Produced standalone problem/state semantics, capability contracts, resource model, invariants, assurance/change protocol, unresolved choices, traceability and an explicit exit audit.

## Phase 9 — Clean-sheet architecture

**First-pass candidate generation completed on 2026-08-14. Exit gate: PASS. No architecture selected.** Four organization families remain active: hierarchical explicit allocation; distributed event-driven local control; tightly integrated predictive computation with external evidence/authority; developmental/variant-generating organization. Phase 9 produced candidate specifications, 22 discriminating experiments and an unresolved design ledger.

## Phase 10 — Experimental reconstruction

**Active. Tier-1 experiment implementation checkpoint reached on 2026-08-14.**

The smallest research organism now contains all six Tier-1 discriminator shapes:

1. E01 hierarchical vs distributed operation allocation;
2. E02 integrated vs heterogeneous learned computation;
3. E03 direct-address evidence vs compressed evolving state;
4. E04 internal representation/interface format;
5. E05 fixed vs adaptive computation;
6. E09 immediate durable learning vs staged consolidation.

E01/E02/E04 now have an initial 12-seed aggregate sweep. Replicated first-pass patterns are: coordination topology has a dependency-density trade-off; learned sharing has a transfer/interference frontier controlled by task relatedness; representation requirements are heterogeneous across approximate values, exact identity, protocol stability, integrity and auditability.

**No design-ledger choice is promoted yet.** Before selecting a core organization, require a second task family, changed resource regime, mechanism-targeted failure/ablation, multiple seeds and matched state/compute/communication accounting.

High-value next work: E01 second environment plus communication-cost sweep; E02 nonlinear/compositional environment plus partially shared alternatives; E04 bandwidth/fidelity sweep plus learned continuous channel with exact side state; adaptive E09 under hidden volatility; aggregate E03/E05/E09; then Tier-2 E06/E07 without freezing unresolved Tier-1 boundaries.

Phase-10 exit condition remains: enough replicated experimental evidence to move several high-value design-ledger decisions from `unresolved` to provisional selections with explicit falsifiers.
