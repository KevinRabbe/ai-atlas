# Foundations — Source Registry

This is not a popularity list. Canonical work is included where it establishes a mechanism or bound; counterexamples and negative results are included with equal priority.

## Gaps for the next Foundations pass

The first pass is intentionally broad. Before declaring Foundations saturated, expand primary evidence for:

- PAC-Bayes, algorithmic stability and compression bounds;
- active learning and value of information;
- causal discovery under interventions and nonstationarity;
- partially observable control and belief-state approximations;
- bounded rationality / rational metareasoning;
- exploration with safety constraints;
- compositionality and systematic generalization;
- program synthesis and minimum-description induction;
- energy/thermodynamic limits of computation;
- communication complexity and distributed computation;
- robust decision-making under model misspecification;
- biological credit/memory mechanisms only where they yield experimentally separable computational hypotheses.
