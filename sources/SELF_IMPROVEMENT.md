## First-pass gaps

Before self-improvement is treated as saturated, expand evidence for:

- meta-gradient/learned optimizer systems that improve update rules themselves;
- tool creation and autonomous skill-library evolution;
- memory-policy self-modification over long horizons;
- self-generated curriculum/task discovery under independent grounding;
- long-running open-ended populations with changing objectives;
- self-modification under scarce/expensive evaluators;
- causal attribution of failures to model vs harness vs data vs evaluator;
- trusted-root migration and formally verified self-update protocols;
- self-improvement of world models/perception in interactive environments;
- lifetime-cost accounting for patch accumulation and architecture churn;
- controlled studies of recursive/meta-mutation depth.
