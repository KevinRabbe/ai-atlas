## Gaps

- quantitative value-of-computation estimation for heterogeneous agent operations;
- preference/objective drift over long-lived interactions;
- authority-aware conflict resolution across user, policy and environment objectives;
- multi-objective learning with changing scalarization/constraints;
- calibrated uncertainty over latent objectives in high-dimensional domains;
- resource-rational policies robust to hardware/model cost changes;
- distributed/local metacontrol and communication costs;
- interaction between objective uncertainty and self-improvement;
- methods that preserve Pareto alternatives without combinatorial explosion.
