## Expansion targets

- formal metareasoning/value-of-computation controllers;
- process vs outcome verification at frontier scale;
- latent/executable reasoning comparisons;
- agent trajectory/event-stream representations and recovery;
- tool selection under uncertain latency/reliability;
- proof assistants/formal solvers as inference substrates;
- multi-agent communication compression and topology;
- adversarial evaluator exploitation under increasing search pressure;
- operation-selection policies learned end-to-end;
- exact lifetime cost comparison of inference search vs consolidation into skills/weights.
