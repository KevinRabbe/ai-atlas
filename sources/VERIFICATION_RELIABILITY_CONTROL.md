## First-pass gaps

Before Phase 5 is treated as saturated, expand evidence for:

- formal software/hardware verification outside theorem proving;
- robust statistics/OOD detection and distribution-free risk control;
- scalable oversight, debate and weak-to-strong supervision beyond current toy analogues;
- control protocols under stronger adaptive/colluding adversaries;
- secure memory/provenance systems and capability-based execution;
- mechanistic monitoring with adversarially aware probes;
- post-deployment incident detection and recovery;
- property-based/fuzz testing for autonomous agents;
- quantitative assurance budgets that combine consequence, privilege, irreversibility and evaluator uncertainty;
- long-running regression/self-modification benchmarks with hidden rotating test suites.
